Company Overview

Chantier Naval de l’Océan Indien (CNOI) is a Mauritian shipyard founded in 1997, operating from the industrial zone of Mer Rouge, Port Louis. CNOI focuses on ship repair, refit, and mid‑size newbuilds (workboats, fishing vessels, patrol/craft). The company is majority‑owned by IBL Ltd with additional holdings by Banyan Holdings and Naval Investment Co.

Capabilities & Facilities

  • Dry docking, hoist/slipway, outfitting quay, steel/aluminium fabrication, mechanical/electrical workshops
  • Project management and planning for concurrent repair and construction programs
  • Quality, HSE and class-compliance workflows embedded across projects

Business Model

  • Repairs & refits: recurring demand from regional fleets (Indian Ocean, East Africa)
  • Newbuilds: project-based revenue with milestone billing
  • Aftercare: inspections, warranty items, spares and upgrades

Registry: CHANTIER NAVAL DE L'OCEAN INDIEN LIMITED (C06017475). Auditor: Deloitte. Company Secretary: IBL Management Ltd.

EOS Readiness

Strengths

  • Clear ownership and governance; external audit
  • Experienced technical leadership and workforce depth
  • Financial headroom to invest in change

Risks to Manage

  • Group–subsidiary alignment can slow decisions
  • Scheduling friction between newbuilds and urgent repairs
  • Change fatigue among long‑tenured teams if rollout is not paced

Recommendation: Start with Focus Day + 90‑day rollout, prioritising Scorecard, Rocks, and top‑5 processes.

Strategic Insights & Pain Points

  • Capacity planning: ensure a master schedule that balances dock time, hoist usage and critical path items.
  • Right People/Right Seats: clarify span of control for Operations vs Projects vs Repairs to reduce handoff friction.
  • Process discipline: lock in quality/HSE practices across all jobs; reduce rework and inspection delays.
  • Commercial mix: diversify client segments and geographies to stabilise order book seasonality.
  • IBL interface: define governance rhythm so approvals don’t bottleneck execution.

Meeting Preparation
