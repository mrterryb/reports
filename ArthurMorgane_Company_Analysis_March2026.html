Executive Summary

A 14-year-old multi-shareholder property development vehicle that built and operates Avalon Golf Estate — one of Mauritius's top-two championship golf courses — but whose FY2024 accounts reveal a structurally loss-making operation sitting on a mountain of developer debt with negative equity of MUR 7.1 million.

Arthur & Morgane Ltd (File No. C105063, BRN C11105063) was incorporated on 7 September 2011 as Lignecaliste Property Company Ltd before being renamed. The company is the legal owner and operator of Avalon Golf Estate at Bois Chéri in southern Mauritius — a 220-hectare integrated lifestyle development comprising an 18-hole championship golf course designed by Peter Matkovich, two restaurants (Magic Spoon and The Bunker), a full-service country club, wellness facilities, a real estate morcellement of 314+ residential plots, and ancillary retail and beauty services. The company also operates a real estate agency out of Shop No 3, Newton Tower, Port Louis. Trading publicly as .THE BUNKER AVALON, the entity registered its stated capital at MUR 5,000,000 (500,000 ordinary shares fully paid), with its last annual registration fee paid for 2026. FY2024 financial statements were most recently filed as at 30 June 2024.

Ownership is spread across 14 shareholders — a constellation of individuals and sociétés — with no single controlling party. The largest single holder is Société MCSK at 24.13%, a vehicle associated with Michel Chan Sui Ko, Chairman of the NeXTeracom Group, which served as promoter and marketing agent for the estate. The second-largest shareholder is Gerald Lincoln (23.13%), Country Managing Partner at Ernst & Young Mauritius and co-founder of Lincoln Realty Group, which currently markets unsold residential plots. Both are founding directors. NeXTeracom's registered address (Level 12, Tower 1, Nexteracom Cybercity, Ebene) is also the registered office of Arthur & Morgane, making the NeXTeracom group the de facto institutional backbone of the company. The remaining 52.74% is held across 12 further individual and corporate shareholders, including Csizmadia Daryl Matthew and Lai Wan Loong Andre Young, who joined as directors in September 2022 — suggesting a governance restructure at that time.

Financially, the picture is sharply bifurcated. The operating business generates a strong 54.6% gross profit margin on MUR 149.3M in turnover — consistent with a premium hospitality and golf operation. But administration costs of MUR 87.1M (58.3% of revenue) swamp gross profit, producing an EBIT loss of MUR 5.4M and a pre-tax loss of the same. The anomalous tax expense of MUR 34.4M on a pre-tax loss then produces a reported net loss of MUR 39.7M, wiping equity into negative territory of MUR -7.1M. The balance sheet is dominated by MUR 403.6M of "inventories" and MUR 556.1M of "trade and other receivables" — almost certainly unsold residential plots and intercompany/developer loans rather than operational current assets. Against total liabilities of MUR 1.41 billion, the company is technically insolvent by equity measures, though the asset base (PP&E of MUR 431.4M) is substantial. EOS suitability scores 5.5/10: the complexity, multi-owner structure, and clear operational growing pains make this a legitimate EOS candidate — but the financial distress and governance opacity are gating factors.

Critical Finding — Structural Insolvency Signal & Anomalous Tax Charge
Arthur & Morgane Ltd reports negative total equity of MUR −7,133,067 as at 30 June 2024, meaning total liabilities of MUR 1.41 billion exceed total assets of MUR 1.40 billion. Additionally, a tax expense of MUR 34,355,519 has been booked against a pre-tax loss of MUR 5,380,735 — a combination that is highly unusual and requires explanation. This may reflect deferred tax movements on the large property asset base, prior-year adjustments, or a reporting anomaly. Until this is clarified, the true economic picture of the P&L cannot be considered reliable. Any engagement with this company should begin with a request for the detailed tax note to the FY2024 accounts.

Company Overview

A multi-shareholder Mauritian property development and hospitality company built around one flagship asset — Avalon Golf Estate — with tentacles into real estate agency, wellness, retail, and beauty, all under the trading brand .THE BUNKER AVALON.

Founding and Corporate Record

Arthur & Morgane Ltd was incorporated on 7 September 2011 under the original name Lignecaliste Property Company Ltd, reflecting its initial purpose as a land holding and property development vehicle. The name change to Arthur & Morgane Ltd preceded the 2013 ground-breaking of Avalon Golf Estate, with the project inaugurated in 2015. The company is registered as a Live, Private, Domestic company under File No. C105063 (BRN: C11105063). Its registered office is Level 12, Tower 1, Nexteracom Cybercity, Ebene, Mauritius — co-located with the NeXTeracom Group. Secretary since 9 January 2012 is Chung Kai Shing Michael Francis Y.C, who is simultaneously a shareholder (5.33%), director (appointed 22/12/2011), and CEO of Lincoln Realty Group. Stated share capital is MUR 5,000,000 (500,000 ordinary shares, all fully paid). Annual registration fees are current for 2026. Financial statements have been filed for FY2022, FY2023, and FY2024 (year ended 30 June). No charges are registered against the company at CBRIS. Total project investment in Avalon Golf Estate has been publicly stated at MUR 1.70 billion.

The company acquired 544.78 arpents (approximately 220–230 hectares) of land in Bois Sec from Union Sugar Estate Ltd, transforming former tea and sugar land into an integrated lifestyle development. Construction commenced 1 June 2013 under main contractor PAD & Co Ltd. The estate's three-storey country clubhouse became operational in 2015, with the golf course, restaurants, and residential morcellement following. Residential lots (ranging from MUR 3.9M to MUR 20M) were marketed to both Mauritian families (314 lots) and foreign investors under Mauritius's Real Estate Scheme (approximately 111 RES-eligible units). Over 70% of local plots were reportedly sold by late 2014.

Anomalous Tax Charge — Flag for Immediate Clarification
The company reports a pre-tax loss of MUR 5,380,735 yet books a tax expense of MUR 34,355,519 — producing a net loss of MUR 39,736,254. A tax charge on a loss is unusual. Possible explanations include: (a) large deferred tax liabilities arising from accelerated depreciation on PP&E of MUR 431M; (b) revaluation of deferred tax on property assets; (c) prior-year tax adjustments; or (d) a tax on unrealised gains. Until the tax note is examined, the reported net loss of MUR 39.7M should be treated as indicative only. The pre-tax loss of MUR 5.4M is likely the more operationally meaningful figure.

The 3 Numbers That Matter Most
1. Admin costs at 58.3% of revenue (MUR 87.1M): This single line item is the difference between a profitable premium club and a loss-making one. Reducing admin costs by even 10 percentage points to 48% of revenue would swing the company from an EBIT loss of −MUR 5.4M to an EBIT profit of +MUR 9.5M — a turnaround of MUR 14.9M without a single additional customer.

2. Negative equity of −MUR 7.1M: On a total asset base of MUR 1.40B, this is a thin margin. Any further net losses — whether from operations or additional deferred tax charges — deepen insolvency. Shareholder recapitalisation or a formal restructuring of the MUR 640M NCL is needed to restore balance sheet health.

3. Zero reported finance costs on MUR 640M of long-term debt: This is almost certainly not the economic reality. Finding and understanding the true cost of carrying MUR 640M in long-term obligations is essential before any financial projection or valuation exercise.

Structural Competitive Advantages

1. Location scarcity and physical irreproducibility. Avalon Golf Estate occupies 220 hectares in the south-central highlands of Mauritius — a terrain that no competitor can replicate. The combination of tea-field panoramas, mountain elevation, cooler climate (advantageous for golfers in tropical heat), and helicopter access creates a genuinely differentiated setting. No new comparable site in southern Mauritius is publicly known to be in development.

2. Ranking and brand equity in the Mauritius golf market. The #2 ranking in Mauritius (Top 100 Golf Courses) and 4.7/5 TripAdvisor rating (255 reviews) represent accumulated brand trust that is difficult to displace. Peter Matkovich's design credentials, the championship-length layout (6,318m from the gold tees), and the Links2Golf global reciprocity network give the club genuine international standing. This supports premium green-fee and membership pricing above many local competitors.

3. Vertically integrated lifestyle offering. Few competitors in Mauritius combine a championship golf course with fine dining (350-person capacity), wellness (Bodhi Centre), sports (tennis, volleyball, football), beauty and retail, events/weddings, and residential real estate in a single estate. This vertical integration drives multiple revenue streams from a shared fixed cost base — in theory. In practice, admin cost discipline is needed to realise the margin potential.

Industry Trends and Tailwinds

  • Mauritius tourism at record levels: 1.44M visitors in 2025 with record earnings of MUR 103.4Bn. Hotel occupancy at 84% — driving demand for non-hotel leisure destinations like Avalon for day visitors, corporate events, and golf tourism packages.
  • Golf tourism growth: Global golf tourism CAGR of 7.8% through 2033, driven by rising disposable incomes and wellness-aligned leisure preferences. Mauritius benefits from European and South African source markets with strong golf culture.

Highest-Priority EOS Tools for Arthur & Morgane Ltd

Accountability Chart

The most urgent EOS tool for this company. With 14 shareholders, 9 directors, a professional MD, and a Club Manager, no one can tell you with certainty who owns what. The A/C would force clarity: which 5–6 seats constitute the Leadership Team? Does Ryan Dodds own all operational revenue? Who owns the real estate sales function? Who owns the F&B P&L? Every seat, every responsibility, one owner.

Scorecard

The 58.3% admin-to-revenue ratio exists because no one is watching the right numbers weekly. A Scorecard of 5–15 weekly measurables — green fees collected, covers served, membership revenue, admin cost run-rate, residential plot deposits received — would surface the admin overrun every Monday morning rather than in the annual CBRIS filing. Evidence-based management replacing reactive firefighting.

Rocks (90-Day Priorities)

The company is simultaneously running golf, fine dining, beauty, retail, real estate, events, and art exhibitions. Without quarterly Rocks, every initiative is equally important — which means the admin team is spread across too many fronts. Three to seven company Rocks per quarter (e.g., "Launch 2026 membership tier by April 30," "Reduce admin cost ratio to 50% by Q2") would create the focus the FY2024 numbers show is currently missing.

Vision / Traction Organiser (V/TO)

Is Arthur & Morgane Ltd a golf club that sells land, or a property developer that runs a golf club to protect asset values? The answer determines everything: staffing model, capital allocation, partnership strategy, and exit planning. The V/TO's 10-year picture and 3-year picture exercises would force the 9 directors to reach a written, shared answer — which they visibly have not yet done, given the eclectic mix of registered business activities.

Level 10 Meeting (L10)

A weekly 90-minute structured leadership meeting — same agenda every week — would be transformative in a business where the senior leadership is split between Ebene (NeXTeracom headquarters), Port Louis (EY offices), and Bois Chéri (the estate). The L10 rhythm creates the weekly accountability cadence that prevents admin costs from drifting unchecked for a full fiscal year before anyone formally reviews them.

Issues Solving Track (IDS)

TripAdvisor reviews reveal recurring operational issues: service inconsistency, communication gaps on reservations, waterlogged fairway management, pricing clarity for non-members. These are the symptoms of issues that get raised but never permanently resolved. The IDS framework (Identify, Discuss, Solve) in the L10 meeting structure would build the discipline of permanent resolution over symptomatic firefighting — directly addressing the culture inconsistency flagged in reviews.

Financial and Operational Recommendations

Recommendation 01

Obtain and Review the Full FY2024 Tax Note

Before any commercial engagement, request the detailed notes to the FY2024 accounts — specifically the tax note — to understand the MUR 34.4M charge booked against a pre-tax loss. This is non-negotiable: without this information, the true P&L, the nature of the deferred tax liability (MUR 3.3M on the balance sheet), and the integrity of the net loss figure (MUR 39.7M) cannot be assessed. Any EOS or consulting proposal submitted without this clarity is built on an unreliable financial foundation.

Immediate Priority — Before Any Engagement
Recommendation 02

Identify and Quantify the MUR 640M Non-Current Liability

The "Other NCL" of MUR 640.7M — the largest single item on the balance sheet — is undisclosed. Understanding whether this is shareholder loans (at what interest rate?), third-party developer financing (which institution?), or lot-buyer deposits (repayable on what conditions?) is critical to assessing true solvency and identifying who has economic leverage over the company's strategy. No charges are registered at CBRIS, suggesting either unsecured lending or off-entity structuring. Request a liability schedule with counterparty, terms, and security position for each obligation.

Immediate Priority — Before Any Engagement
Recommendation 03

Target Admin Cost Reduction to 48% of Revenue (from 58.3%)

This single intervention is worth more than any revenue growth strategy. Reducing admin costs by 10 percentage points — from MUR 87.1M to approximately MUR 71.7M (a MUR 15.4M reduction) — would swing EBIT from a loss of −MUR 5.4M to a profit of approximately +MUR 10M. The cost reduction programme should begin with a zero-based budget review of all admin line items, benchmarking against comparable golf and country clubs in the Indian Ocean region. Success metric: admin costs below MUR 72M in FY2025, reported in the next CBRIS filing.

Recommendation 04

Accelerate Residential Lot Sales to Clear Developer Inventory

Selling these lots converts balance sheet debt into equity, reduces the payables overhang (MUR 732M trade payables likely includes lot-buyer deposits and intercompany advances), and enables the company to refinance or repay the MUR 640M NCL. Lincoln Realty Group is already the marketing agent — the action required is an explicit sales acceleration programme with quarterly plot-sales Rocks, pricing review for remaining lots, and a potential international marketing push targeting the South African and European HNW buyer segments via the RES/PDS framework.
